Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Copycat Dance ("the app") is a game. This policy explains what information is used when you play and why.

No account, no personal profile

The app has no sign-up. Your progress (coins, unlocked dancers and stages, scores and settings) is stored only on your device and in your device backups. We do not run servers that receive your game data.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation of what was bought so we can unlock it, but never your payment details, name or Apple Account information.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your Game Center settings.

Advertising

Players without VIP or Remove Ads see ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device information, and how you interact with ads. If you do not allow tracking, ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent using Google's consent tool before any personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice anytime under Settings > Privacy choices. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Diagnostics

Apple may share anonymous crash and performance reports with us if you have chosen to share them with developers in iOS settings.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking: iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Remove ads entirely with VIP or Remove Ads.
  • Delete all game data by deleting the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us.

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date above shows the latest version.
