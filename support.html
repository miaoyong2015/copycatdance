Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed) and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within two business days.

Restore purchases

Open the game, tap the gear (Settings), then Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

Manage or cancel Copycat VIP

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Copycat VIP. You can also tap Manage subscription in the game's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My taps feel late

Bluetooth headphones add audio delay. In Settings, open Timing calibration and tap along to the beat; the game adjusts automatically.

Ad privacy choices

If you are in a region where consent is required, Settings shows Privacy choices, where you can change your ad consent at any time.
